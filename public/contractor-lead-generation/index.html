Built for Contractors Who Run Their Business From the Truck

Most home service contractors do not lose work because they are bad at the job. They lose it because a homeowner searched, found three companies, and hired the first one that answered. When you are on a job site, behind a plow or under a vehicle, that is hard to win by hand.

Our lead generation work for contractors covers both halves of the problem. First we make sure the right homeowners and property managers find you: a fast website with a page for every service, a complete Google Business Profile, local SEO for the towns you serve and, when it makes sense, paid search and Local Services Ads.

Then our SaaS developers connect what happens next. Calls, forms and messages are captured in one place, missed calls get an instant text back, leads are routed to the right person, and follow-ups and review requests go out automatically. You see which channels produce booked jobs, not just traffic.

Contractor Lead Generation FAQs

Common questions from contractors

Do I need a new website to start?

Not always. If your current site loads fast, works on phones and has a page for each service, we can build on it. If it is slow or hard to update, a new contractor site is usually the fastest way to improve lead quality.

How quickly will I see leads?

Paid search and Local Services Ads can start producing calls soon after launch. Local SEO and Google Business Profile improvements build over several months. Most contractors use both: ads for steady volume now and organic search for lower cost over time.

Can you connect to the CRM or field service software I already use?

In most cases, yes. Our developers connect common CRMs and field service tools, and can build custom integrations when a standard connection does not exist.

Do you only work with contractors in Minnesota?

No. We are based in Minneapolis and work with home service contractors nationwide.

What does contractor lead generation cost?

It depends on your trade, the size of your service area and whether paid ads are included. Call us and we will give you a clear scope and price after looking at your current setup.
