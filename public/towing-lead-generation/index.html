Towing Customers Call the First Company That Answers

Almost nobody plans a tow. A driver is stuck on a shoulder or in a parking lot, searches on their phone, and calls the first company that looks close and available. If that call rings out, they hang up and call the next one. For a towing company, visibility and answering speed are the whole game.

Our towing lead generation is phone-first. Your Google Business Profile, local SEO and mobile site are built so you appear close to where drivers search, with a large tap-to-call button and clear service details: towing, roadside assistance, lockouts, jump starts, accident recovery, heavy-duty and junk car removal, whichever you offer.

Then we protect every call. Call tracking shows which channels produce tows, missed calls get an instant text asking for location and vehicle details, and ads are scheduled around your dispatch capacity so you are not paying for calls you cannot take.

Where Towing Leads Come From

Channels tuned for towing companies

  • Google Business Profile and Maps

    Accurate categories, hours, service area and photos of your trucks, so you show up in map results near the driver.

  • Mobile Site With Tap-to-Call

    A fast mobile site where calling you takes one tap, with pages for each service and area you cover.

  • Call-Focused Search Ads

    Ads built for phone calls, scheduled around your dispatch capacity and focused on areas your trucks can reach quickly.

  • Missed-Call Text Back

    Every missed call gets a text asking for location and vehicle details, so the driver waits for you instead of calling someone else.

  • Call Tracking and Recording

    See which channels produce paid tows and review calls to improve how dispatch handles them.

  • Reviews After Every Job

    Automatic review requests after each tow build the ratings drivers check before they call.

Towing Lead Generation FAQs

Questions from towing companies

Do you handle 24/7 towing companies?

Yes. Ads, hours and automation are set up around when you actually dispatch, including overnight and weekend coverage.

Can you help us get more private-pay calls?

Yes. Search visibility and call ads bring drivers directly to you, and call tracking shows how many of those calls become paid tows.

We cover several cities. How is that handled?

We set up service-area pages and Google Business Profile service areas for the cities you cover, and keep ads focused on places your trucks can reach quickly.

Can you track which calls turn into jobs?

Yes. Calls are tracked by source, and dispatch can mark outcomes so reporting shows cost per paid tow, not just cost per call.
